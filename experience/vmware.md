# VMware

- **Role:** Member of Technical Staff III
- **Team:** Tanzu, Bitnami content team
- **Dates:** Jun 2022 → Nov 2023
- **Location:** Remote, A Coruña
- **Type:** Full-time
- **Stack:** Docker, Kubernetes, Helm, Go, Kafka, Grafana

The Bitnami content team builds and maintains the containers and Helm charts of the Bitnami Application Catalog and the Tanzu Application Catalog.

- I was one of three engineers who created and released new assets for both catalogs: the build recipe, the container and its logic, and the Helm chart with its own Kubernetes scenario. Assets I introduced include Jaeger, Apache Flink, Fluent Bit and OpenSearch.
- I proposed and started a Go tool to observe the catalog's pipelines and triage their failures, and led the small team of four that built it.
- I was part of the support team for Bitnami's containers and charts, answering the community on the public GitHub repositories: databases, messaging with Kafka and RabbitMQ, observability, monitoring and tracing.

Bitnami's culture was very transparent, and working close to the productivity and cross-team groups taught me a lot about how decisions get made and how people are managed.
