# DEUS AI

- **Role:** Technical Lead, E-Racing
- **Dates:** Mar 2022 → Jun 2022
- **Location:** Remote, A Coruña
- **Type:** Full-time
- **Stack:** Python, machine learning, Scrum

E-Racing started as an idea of mine that the company turned into its internship project: an application that brings data and analytics to racing teams, applying machine learning to sim racing telemetry and session metrics.

- I was the interns' technical lead and project manager: I reviewed their code, planned the work and introduced them to Scrum.
- The project went through a design sprint that the company later published.
