# INDITEX

- **Role:** Automation & Release Engineer
- **Team:** Engineering Productivity
- **Company:** Torus Software Solutions
- **Dates:** Aug 2020 → Dec 2021
- **Location:** Remote, A Coruña
- **Type:** Full-time
- **Stack:** Java, Maven, Node.js, GitHub Actions, Jenkins, Gitflow

The Engineering Productivity team industrialised the workflows and repositories of hundreds of INDITEX teams, from development to release. In order, this is what I did there:

- Standardised the Maven builds and quality gates (SpotBugs, style guides, linters) of the company's Java projects, and maintained the templates and archetypes per language.
- Defined the development and release flows for the whole company: Gitflow, versioning and the path to deployment.
- Worked on a Java system that opened update pull requests across some 200 repositories, a Dependabot for everything.
- Became technical owner and product owner of Changelog Drafter, the Node.js tool that versioned and wrote the changelog of hundreds of artifacts following SemVer and Keep a Changelog. I owned its roadmap and its releases, and used it to bring teams into the productivity initiatives.
- Designed from scratch a Node.js tool that ran on every pull request in the company to validate conventions: branching and release strategy, pull request format and the link to its Jira ticket.
- Coordinated two people and worked with product managers and product owners across the company to get our flows adopted.
