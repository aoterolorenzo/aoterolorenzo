# PayLoBot

- **Dates:** 2014 → 2024
- **Status:** Private
- **Stack:** Python, Java, Go, Amazon Cognito

An automation that gets through the waiting queues of high-demand ticket sales, such as Tomorrowland, where the odds of getting a ticket are around 4%.

It has been rebuilt year after year as the ticketing platform added defences: Amazon Cognito authentication, anti-bot systems and captchas. The first versions were in Python and Java; the last one, TML_TBot, is in Go.

It got us tickets every single year it ran.
